What is AstroAtlas?

Everyone is born on a specific day, at a specific place. AstroAtlas uses that one fact — your birth date, time, and place — to draw a kind of map, the same way scientists can calculate exactly where the planets were on any day in history.

We use that map as a mirror, not a prediction. It gives you plain-language ideas about what comes naturally to you, and where things get harder — then a small, real-world thing to try each day, like breathing exercises, journaling, or a short walk. It's not magic and it doesn't tell the future — it's a structured way to notice yourself, and the people you care about, a little more clearly.
